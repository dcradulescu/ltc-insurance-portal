This document contains comprehensive video production scripts for the SC Visiting Angels LTC Insurance Portal. Each script includes detailed specifications for camera work, lighting, talent, graphics, animations, and on-screen text.

## VIDEO 1: HOME PAGE - "Welcome to LTC Navigator" (60 seconds)

### Video Specifications
- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Format:** 16:9 widescreen (1920x1080)
- **Frame Rate:** 24fps (cinematic feel)
- **Color Grade:** Warm, inviting pastels (soft blues, teals, greens)
- **Music:** Calm, uplifting background score (instrumental)

**Camera Setup:**
- **Lens:** 50mm (standard, natural perspective)

- **Focus:** Soft focus background, sharp foreground

**Foreground Content:**
- Close-up of hands holding a policy document
- Hands are warm-toned, wearing neutral clothing
- Document is clearly visible with "Long-Term Care Insurance Policy" header

**Background:**
- Soft-focus home interior (living room with natural light)
- Warm sunlight streaming through window
- Pastel blue/green wall color
- Blurred family photos on shelf

- Font: Aptos, 48pt, white with slight shadow
- Animation: Fade in + subtle scale (1.0 to 1.05)
- Duration: 4 seconds (disappears at 6 seconds)

**Voiceover (Warm, professional female voice):**
"If you're facing a long-term care insurance claim, you're not alone. Many families feel confused and overwhelmed by the process."

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### General Guidelines for All Videos

**Talent:**
- Diverse representation (age 40-70, various ethnicities)
- Warm, professional demeanor
- Natural, conversational speaking style
- Authentic emotions (not overly dramatic)

**Lighting:**
- Warm color temperature (3200-4000K)
- Soft, diffused lighting (avoid harsh shadows)
- Natural light where possible
- Fill lights to reduce shadows
- Practical lights (lamps, window light) for authenticity

**Audio:**
- Professional voiceover talent (warm, clear, conversational)
- Background music: royalty-free, instrumental
- Sound effects: subtle, professional (not cartoonish)
- Voiceover should be 60-70% of total audio mix
- Music should support but not overpower voiceover

**Color Grading:**
- Warm pastels (blues, teals, greens, yellows)
- Consistent color palette across all videos
- Skin tones: warm, natural
- Shadows: soft, not crushed
- Highlights: bright but not blown out

**Typography:**
- Font: Aptos (as specified)
- No italics anywhere
- Clear hierarchy (sizes: 52pt for main, 32-40pt for subheads, 24-28pt for body)
- High contrast (white or dark text on colored backgrounds)
- Consistent spacing and alignment

**Animations:**
- Smooth, professional (not jerky)
- Fade in/out: 0.3-0.5 seconds
- Slide animations: 0.4-0.6 seconds
- Scale animations: subtle (1.0 to 1.05 or 1.1)
- Staggered animations: 0.2-0.5 seconds between items
- Checkmark animations: satisfying but quick (0.3 seconds)

**Pacing:**
- Allow time for viewers to read text (minimum 2 seconds per text element)
- Voiceover should match on-screen text timing
- Transitions: 0.3-0.5 seconds
- No abrupt cuts (use fades or smooth transitions)

**Call-to-Action:**
- Clear, prominent at end of each video
- Button-style text with background color
- Consistent messaging across videos
- Encourage next step in user journey

**Accessibility:**
- Captions for all voiceover
- High contrast text
- Clear, simple language
- Audio descriptions for key visual elements

### Technical Specifications

**Video Format:**
- Codec: H.264 (MP4)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (16:9)
- Frame Rate: 24fps
- Bitrate: 5-8 Mbps
- Audio: AAC, 128 kbps, 48 kHz

**File Naming Convention:**
- `LTC_[PageName]_[Duration]sec_[Version].mp4`
- Example: `LTC_Home_60sec_v1.mp4`
