### CHARACTER DESIGN
- Professional 3D character (staff member or representative)
- Warm, approachable appearance
- Expressive facial animations

### AUDIO SPECIFICATIONS
- Professional voiceover (warm, clear, helpful tone)
- CRITICAL: NO echo, NO reverb, NO room ambience
- Background music: Friendly, approachable instrumental
- Subtle sound effects:
  - Card appearance sounds
  - Phone icon animation sound
  - Email notification sound
  - Map pin drop sound
  - Smooth whoosh for transitions
- Voiceover: 60-70% of total audio mix
- Music: Supporting but not overpowering

### ANIMATION SPECIFICATIONS
- Smooth transitions (0.3-0.5 seconds)
- Text animations: Fade in/scale effects
- Card animations: Slide in with 3D rotation
- Character animations: Natural, believable
- Particle effects: Subtle, professional
- Camera movements: Smooth, gradual
- Timing: Allow viewers to read text (minimum 2 seconds per element)

### TYPOGRAPHY
- Font: Aptos (no italics)
- Clear hierarchy (52pt titles, 32-44pt subheads, 24-28pt body)
- High contrast text
- Consistent spacing and alignment

### PACING
- Allow time for viewers to read text
- Voiceover matches on-screen text timing
- Smooth transitions between contact methods
- Closing feels warm and inviting

### CALL-TO-ACTION
- Warm, encouraging tone
- Emphasizes availability and support
- Invites contact through multiple channels

### ACCESSIBILITY
- Captions for all voiceover
- High contrast text (minimum 4.5:1 ratio)
- Clear, simple language
- No flashing or strobing effects
