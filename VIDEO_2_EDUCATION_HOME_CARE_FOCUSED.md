## VOICEOVER SCRIPT (FULL)

"Many families worry: 'How will we afford home care if we need it?' That's exactly what your Long-Term Care insurance is designed for. It helps pay for the care you need right in your own home. Let me show you how it works.

Home care means having a trained caregiver come to your home to help you with daily activities. They help with bathing, dressing, preparing meals, mobility, and other tasks you might need assistance with. Your LTC insurance is designed to help pay for these services so you can stay in your home with the support you need.

Your Daily Benefit Amount is the maximum your insurance will pay each day toward home care costs. So if your policy pays a certain amount per day, you can calculate how much help you'll get each month. This is really important because it tells you exactly how much your insurance will contribute toward paying for your home care. Let me show you an example: if your daily benefit is a certain amount, multiply that by 30 days, and you'll see what your insurance covers each month for home care. This helps you plan and budget for the care you need at home.

Now, there's something important to understand about paying for home care: the Elimination Period. This is the number of days you'll need to pay for home care yourself before your insurance starts helping. Common elimination periods are 30, 60, or 90 days. I know that sounds challenging, but here's the good news: knowing this in advance helps you plan. You can save for this period, or discuss payment options with your home care provider. Once this period is over, your insurance kicks in to help pay for your home care. That's why understanding your elimination period is so important for planning your home care budget.

Your Maximum Benefit Period tells you how long your insurance will help pay for home care. Most policies cover 3 to 5 years of home care, though some offer unlimited coverage. This is really important because it helps you understand how long your insurance will support your home care needs. If you need home care for many years, this information helps you plan and understand what to expect financially.

Now you understand how your LTC insurance helps pay for home care. You know what your daily benefit means, how the elimination period works, and how long your insurance will support you. You're ready to move forward with confidence. Let's gather your policy details so we can create your personalized home care strategy."
