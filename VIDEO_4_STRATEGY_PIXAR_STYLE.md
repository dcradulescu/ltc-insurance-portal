## PRODUCTION NOTES

### ANIMATION STYLE
- Match character design and animation style to previous videos
- Smooth, professional 3D animations
- Empowering, organized tone
- Timeline animation should be smooth and progressive
- Celebration animations should feel professional and appropriate

### COLOR PALETTE
- **Primary Colors:** Teal (#34B1C4), Blue (#0692D7), Warm Green
- **Accent Colors:** Soft Yellow, Soft Orange
- **Neutrals:** Warm White, Soft Gray, Charcoal
- **Lighting:** Warm color temperature (3200-4000K equivalent)

### CHARACTER DESIGN
- Consistent with previous videos
- Friendly, approachable 3D character
- Expressive facial animations
- Natural body movements
- Professional appearance

### AUDIO SPECIFICATIONS
- Professional voiceover (warm, clear, empowering tone)
- CRITICAL: NO echo, NO reverb, NO room ambience
- Background music: Inspiring, motivational instrumental
- Subtle sound effects:
  - Checkmark "ding" sound
  - Timeline growth sound
  - Approval notification sound
  - Celebration sounds (not cartoonish)
  - Smooth whoosh for transitions
- Voiceover: 60-70% of total audio mix

### ANIMATION SPECIFICATIONS
- Smooth transitions (0.3-0.5 seconds)
- Text animations: Fade in/scale effects
- Timeline animation: Smooth, progressive growth
- Checkmark animations: Satisfying but quick
- Character animations: Natural, believable
- Particle effects: Subtle, professional
- Camera movements: Smooth, gradual
- Timing: Allow viewers to read text (minimum 2 seconds per element)

### TYPOGRAPHY
- Font: Aptos (no italics)
- Clear hierarchy (52pt titles, 32-44pt subheads, 24-28pt body)
- High contrast text
- Consistent spacing and alignment

### PACING
- Allow time for viewers to read text
- Voiceover matches on-screen text timing
- Smooth transitions between phases
- Timeline should feel progressive and organized

### CALL-TO-ACTION
- Empowerment message at end
- Supportive, encouraging tone
- Prepares viewer for next steps

### ACCESSIBILITY
- Captions for all voiceover
- High contrast text (minimum 4.5:1 ratio)
- Clear, simple language
- No flashing or strobing effects

## VOICEOVER SCRIPT (FULL)

"You've completed your policy information. Now comes the most important part: your personalized strategy. This is a step-by-step roadmap designed specifically for your situation, based on your unique policy and care needs.

Phase 1 is your Initial Assessment, covering days 1 through 7. During this phase, you'll review your policy details, gather medical documentation, schedule an initial consultation, and prepare questions for your insurance company.

Phase 2 is Claim Initiation, days 8 through 21. Contact your insurance company, request claim forms and requirements, provide preliminary medical information, obtain your claim number, and confirm what documentation your care provider needs to submit.

Phase 3 is Documentation and Submission, days 22 through 45. Compile all required medical documentation, coordinate with your care provider for records, organize and label everything, submit your complete claim package, and get a submission confirmation.

Phase 4 is Follow-up and Approval, starting day 46. Monitor your claim status, respond to any insurance company requests, provide additional documentation if needed, receive your approval notification, and finally, begin receiving your benefits.

You've got this. We're here to support you every step of the way. Your personalized strategy is your roadmap to success."
