- Voiceover should be 60-70% of total audio mix
- Music should support but not overpower voiceover

- Warm pastels (blues, teals, greens, yellows)
- Consistent color palette across all videos
- Skin tones: warm, natural
- Shadows: soft, not crushed
- Highlights: bright but not blown out

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Font: Aptos (as specified)
- No italics anywhere
- Clear hierarchy (sizes: 52pt for main, 32-40pt for subheads, 24-28pt for body)
- High contrast (white or dark text on colored backgrounds)
- Consistent spacing and alignment

ANIMATIONS:
- Smooth, professional (not jerky)
- Fade in/out: 0.3-0.5 seconds
- Slide animations: 0.4-0.6 seconds
- Scale animations: subtle (1.0 to 1.05 or 1.1)
- Staggered animations: 0.2-0.5 seconds between items
- Checkmark animations: satisfying but quick (0.3 seconds)

PACING:
- Allow time for viewers to read text (minimum 2 seconds per text element)
- Voiceover should match on-screen text timing
- Transitions: 0.3-0.5 seconds
- No abrupt cuts (use fades or smooth transitions)

CALL-TO-ACTION:
- Clear, prominent at end of each video
- Button-style text with background color
- Consistent messaging across videos
- Encourage next step in user journey

ACCESSIBILITY:
- Captions for all voiceover
- High contrast text
- Clear, simple language
- Audio descriptions for key visual elements
