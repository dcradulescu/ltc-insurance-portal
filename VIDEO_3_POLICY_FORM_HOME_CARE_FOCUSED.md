# VIDEO 3: POLICY FORM PAGE - "Getting Your Home Care Claim Started" (75 seconds)
## 3D ANIMATED PIXAR STYLE - HOME CARE FOCUSED

---

## VIDEO OVERVIEW

This video shows families how to complete the policy form to start their home care insurance claim. Every step is framed around getting their home care approved and paid for. The family learns how to gather information and submit their claim so they can access home care services.

## VOICEOVER SCRIPT (FULL)

"This form is your key to getting your home care covered by insurance. I know it looks overwhelming, but every piece of information you provide helps us get your home care approved and paid for. Let's walk through it together, step by step.

First, gather your insurance information. You'll need your insurance policy document, your ID card, your policy number, and your carrier's contact information. This information helps us verify your home care coverage and start the claim process. Don't worry if you can't find everything right away—we can help you locate it.

Now let's fill out your home care claim form. This form tells us about your specific home care situation. You'll provide your name, policy number, the type of home care you need, your current care situation, and relevant medical information. Be specific about your home care needs—this helps us verify that you qualify for benefits and ensures your claim is processed quickly. You can take your time filling this out. There's no rush. If you have questions about any field, we're here to help.

You can also upload supporting documents that strengthen your home care claim. These might include medical records, care assessments, doctor's recommendations, or recent care invoices. These documents help us understand your home care needs and approve your claim faster. Upload whatever you have—it all helps.

You're ready! Click submit to send your home care claim. Once you submit, we'll review everything and contact you within 5 to 7 business days. You're on your way to getting your home care approved and starting the care you need at home."
