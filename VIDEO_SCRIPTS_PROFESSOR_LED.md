## PRODUCTION GUIDELINES

### Talent Direction - The Professor
- **Age:** 60s-70s, warm and grandfatherly
- **Demeanor:** Patient, knowledgeable, genuinely caring about helping families
- **Voice:** Calm, clear, reassuring, with natural warmth
- **Movement:** Gestures naturally, moves around the space to engage the audience, sits at eye level with family members
- **Clothing:** Professional but approachable - blazer, dress shirt, tie (warm colors like burgundy or teal)
- **Expressions:** Warm smile, engaged eye contact, nodding in understanding

### Talent Direction - The Family
- **Rose (Grandmother):** Warm, slightly concerned at first, becomes more confident as she learns
- **Michael (Grandfather):** Supportive, asks practical questions, protective of Rose
- **Elena (Daughter):** Organized, detail-oriented, takes notes
- **David (Son):** Analytical, asks strategic questions, wants to understand the numbers
- **Sarah (Daughter):** Creative, asks about practical implementation, wants to make things work
- **All Family Members:** Gradually shift from worried/confused to confident/empowered

### Lighting Specifications
- **Overall Tone:** Warm, inviting, professional
- **Color Temperature:** 3200K-3500K (warm white, like natural daylight)
- **Key Light:** Soft, diffused light from the front-left, creating gentle shadows
- **Fill Light:** Soft fill from the right to reduce harsh shadows
- **Practical Lights:** Warm lamps in the office/classroom setting
- **Background:** Slightly darker than subjects to create depth
- **No Harsh Shadows:** All shadows should be soft and diffused

### Audio Specifications
- **The Professor's Voiceover:** Clear, warm, professional - NO echo, NO reverb
- **Family Members' Dialogue:** Natural, conversational, clear
- **Background Music:** Soft, uplifting, instrumental (piano or strings)
- **Sound Effects:** Subtle, professional (paper shuffling, pen writing, soft chimes for animations)
- **Overall Mix:** Voiceover at -18dB, music at -24dB, sound effects at -30dB
- **CRITICAL:** NO reverb, NO echo, NO hollow spaces in audio

### Color Grading
- **Palette:** Warm pastels - teals, blues, soft greens, warm yellows, soft grays
- **Saturation:** Slightly desaturated for a professional, calming feel
- **Contrast:** Soft, not harsh
- **Highlights:** Warm, never blown out
- **Shadows:** Warm, never too dark
- **Overall Tone:** Inviting, trustworthy, professional

### Typography Standards
- **Font:** Aptos (no italics anywhere)
- **Sizes:** 
  - Main titles: 48-54pt
  - Section headers: 36-42pt
  - Body text: 24-28pt
  - Small text: 18-20pt
- **Color:** Navy blue (#1B3A5C) or teal (#4A9FB5)
- **Background:** White or very light gray
- **Animations:** Text appears with smooth fade-in, stays on screen 3-5 seconds

### Animation Specifications
- **Style:** Smooth, professional, not cartoonish
- **Transitions:** Dissolves and fades, no jarring cuts
- **Timing:** 24fps for cinematic feel
- **Easing:** Ease-in and ease-out for all animations
- **Emphasis:** Subtle scale animations for important information
- **Celebration Moments:** Checkmarks, stars, and subtle sparkles (not overdone)

### Pacing and Timing
- **Video 1 (60 sec):** 5 scenes, 12 sec per scene average
- **Video 2 (75 sec):** 5 scenes, 15 sec per scene average
- **Video 3 (90 sec):** 5 scenes, 18 sec per scene average
- **Video 4 (60 sec):** 4 scenes, 15 sec per scene average
- **Dialogue Pacing:** Conversational, not rushed, with natural pauses
- **Scene Transitions:** 0.5-1 second dissolves between scenes

### Accessibility Requirements
- **Captions:** All dialogue and on-screen text must be captioned
- **Audio Description:** Optional audio description track for visually impaired viewers
- **Color Contrast:** All text must have sufficient contrast (WCAG AA standard minimum)
- **No Flashing:** No content flashes more than 3 times per second

### Technical Specifications
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (16:9 widescreen)
- **Frame Rate:** 24fps (cinematic)
- **Codec:** H.264
- **Bitrate:** 5-8 Mbps for high quality
- **Audio:** 48kHz, 16-bit, stereo
- **File Format:** MP4
- **Color Space:** Rec. 709
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9

### Production Timeline
- **Week 1-2:** Concept development, storyboarding, character design refinement
- **Week 3-4:** Voice recording, music composition
- **Week 5-6:** 3D animation production
- **Week 7:** Color grading, audio mixing, final edits, captions
