## Added in checkpoint 10 — calculator audit and corrections

- Audited every input, output and conditional branch of the interactive care calculator against the implementation, independently worked examples, and NAIC/ACL/state-regulator insurance guidance. The old calculator was not reliable for planning: waiver savings reduced benefit consumption, inflation used the wrong base for total-maximum riders, the chart described a different growth rate, and monthly caps, elimination periods and eligibility were ignored.
- Replaced duplicated component arithmetic with the shared, directly tested `shared/careCalculator.ts` engine. Reimbursement is limited by eligible expenses, applicable daily/calendar-month caps and the actual remaining pool. Dates use actual weekdays, calendar months and leap years. Integer-cent accounting includes the partial final claim and stops at exhaustion.
- Separated premium payments from care expenses and insurer claims. Waiver requires confirmation it is active for the premium month reviewed and does not extend the pool. Care-cost inflation is an explicit assumption. Verified simple/compound riders specify the increase date and whether caps, total maximum or both grow; simple inflation uses original amounts. No exhausted benefit restoration is implied.
- Added strict conditional validation, eligibility and policy-term confirmations, current versus remaining benefit amounts, prior monthly usage and calendar/service elimination counting. Unsupported policy types or terms block an estimate. This model does not interpret actual contracts or automatically determine claim eligibility. Five-year results do not infer an unmodeled duration beyond the horizon.
- Preserved the portal theme while replacing misleading results with separate care/insurance/household totals, a stacked monthly chart and a complete monthly balance table. No fabricated dollar defaults; policy changes reset the scenario, and edits hide stale results. Hours within a daily cap are rounded down so $250 ÷ $60 never claims 4.17 fully covered hours. Intake and strategy now accept monthly-only limits.

## Changes in checkpoint 6

- Task documents now offers “Use an existing document.” Only unassigned account documents with a compatible policy and strategy are eligible. A document can have one task association; it cannot be attached to multiple tasks simultaneously.
- “Detach from task” preserves the uploaded file, its private download access, and its policy association. It removes the task and strategy association. This is separate from removing portal access.
- Attach and detach endpoints require authentication and account ownership. Attaching also validates the current strategy task and policy. Existing associations cannot be silently moved; updates compare the association read from the database so concurrent changes fail with an explicit conflict. Retrying a completed attach is idempotent.
- Available-document failures expose retry controls. Attach failures retain the selection; detach failures retain the file. Task empty-state wording now says “No documents attached.”
- No schema migration, production database change, external messages, live object-storage operation, or deployment was performed.
